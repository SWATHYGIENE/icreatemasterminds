THE JOURNEY CIRCLE PROGRAM

A little accountability.
A lot of possibility.

Most small business owners have bought the course, hired the agency and sat through the webinar. What they rarely have is a few peers who know their town, will hold them to what they said they'd do, and have nothing to gain from steering them wrong.

That's what a circle is. It isn't coaching from a stage. It's six to ten owners in non-competing businesses, working through the same playbook together, with one person keeping the room on track. That person could be you.

A RHYTHM THAT MOVES YOU FORWARD

Make room for progress.

A Journey Circle is six to ten owners from businesses that don't compete. Each month has the same rhythm.

  1. Working session

    In person, 2 hours, first week of the month

    Every member picks one system to build this month. The leader runs the session from our meeting kit, and members leave with the automation set up in their own workspace, not a to-do list.

  2. Check-in

    Video call, 45 minutes, third week

    Did it go live? What happened? Members report their numbers to the group. Anyone stuck gets a fix before the next session.

  3. Referral hour

    Optional, as the group decides

    Because nobody in the room competes, the members become each other's best source of customers. Many circles end each session by swapping introductions.

The playbook: four places profit leaks out

Circles work through the Route, a four-stage playbook built from fifteen-plus years of running a service business. Each stage ships with ready-made automations in GoHighLevel.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Stop losing the leads you already get: missed calls, web forms nobody answered, quotes that never got followed up.

  2. 2

    Convert

    Respond in minutes instead of days, and turn more estimates into booked work with follow-up that runs itself.

  3. 3

    Keep

    Turn one-time jobs into repeat and recurring customers with reminders, service plans and review requests.

  4. 4

    Multiply

    Build referral partnerships with the other owners in your circle and in your town, and track every introduction.

Find your circle’s meeting space

A shared memory for your circle.

Members can revisit the video, decisions and next steps. Upload working documents and presentations, or create named breakout rooms with their own discussions, files, video meetings and archives. The meeting host uploads recordings after the call.

Recordings require participant consent and are visible to current circle members, including people who join later, and administrators. Each category and subcircle keeps its own archive. These free category spaces are separate from the facilitated Journey Circle program.

Questions people ask

Can each circle share documents and use breakout rooms?

Yes. Every circle has a document library for PDFs, office documents, spreadsheets, presentations, text and images. Members can create named breakout rooms with separate discussions, files and video meetings. All members of the parent circle can access its breakouts; they are focused working spaces, not a separate private membership.

Can our category circle hold and archive video meetings?

Yes. Join your category circle and open Meetings & recordings. A member can schedule a call using an existing video-service link. The host records with participant consent, uploads the MP4 or WebM video, and adds meeting notes. Only that circle’s current members and administrators can access the archive. Built-in video with automatic cloud recording is available after the platform connects its video provider.

Are category circles the same as Journey Circles?

No. Category circles are free collaboration spaces connected to Agrarian Exchange marketplace categories and subcategories. Journey Circles are a separate facilitated program with member dues and leader certification. Joining a free category circle never enrolls you in the paid program.

What happens when I suggest a category?

A suggestion made on Agrarian Exchange or iCreateMasterminds goes to an administrator. After approval, the marketplace category appears on Agrarian Exchange and the matching circle or subcircle appears on iCreateMasterminds. Their names and parent connections stay in sync; private discussions keep their original audience.

What does a circle leader actually do?

You recruit members, run the monthly working session and check-in using our meeting kit, and keep people accountable for what they committed to. You don't write content or build automations; that's already done.

Do I have to be a coach or consultant?

No. Most of our leaders run a business of their own. What matters is that other owners respect you and you're willing to run a structured meeting twice a month.

What do members get for their dues?

A seat in the circle, the Route playbook, and a GoHighLevel workspace with the automations for each stage ready to switch on.

What if I can't fill a circle?

Training covers how to recruit your first members from people you already know. A circle can start with as few as four. We'll also look at your market on the call, and if we don't think it can support a circle, we'll tell you.

Can I join without leading?

Yes. Apply as a member and we'll match you with a circle near you, or put you on the list for when one forms.

Is it tax deductible?

Business education and software costs often are, but it depends on your situation. Ask your accountant.

What happens after I apply?

We read every application. If it looks like a fit, we'll text or email to book a call about your market and goals. If it doesn't, we'll tell you that too.
