How a Journey Circle runs

A Journey Circle is six to ten owners from businesses that don't compete. Each month has the same rhythm.

  1. Working session

    In person, 2 hours, first week of the month

    Every member picks one system to build this month. The leader runs the session from our meeting kit, and members leave with the automation set up in their own workspace, not a to-do list.

  2. Check-in

    Video call, 45 minutes, third week

    Did it go live? What happened? Members report their numbers to the group. Anyone stuck gets a fix before the next session.

  3. Referral hour

    Optional, as the group decides

    Because nobody in the room competes, the members become each other's best source of customers. Many circles end each session by swapping introductions.

The playbook: four places profit leaks out

Circles work through the Route, a four-stage playbook built from fifteen-plus years of running a service business. Each stage ships with ready-made automations in GoHighLevel.

  3. 3

    Multiply

    Build referral partnerships with the other owners in your circle and in your town, and track every introduction.

Questions people ask

What does a circle leader actually do?

You recruit members, run the monthly working session and check-in using our meeting kit, and keep people accountable for what they committed to. You don't write content or build automations; that's already done.

Do I have to be a coach or consultant?

No. Most of our leaders run a business of their own. What matters is that other owners respect you and you're willing to run a structured meeting twice a month.

What do members get for their dues?

A seat in the circle, the Route playbook, and a GoHighLevel workspace with the automations for each stage ready to switch on.

What if I can't fill a circle?

Training covers how to recruit your first members from people you already know. A circle can start with as few as four. We'll also look at your market on the call, and if we don't think it can support a circle, we'll tell you.

Can I join without leading?

Yes. Apply as a member and we'll match you with a circle near you, or put you on the list for when one forms.

Is it tax deductible?

Business education and software costs often are, but it depends on your situation. Ask your accountant.

What happens after I apply?

We read every application. If it looks like a fit, we'll text or email to book a call about your market and goals. If it doesn't, we'll tell you that too.
