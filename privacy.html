Journey Circles is operated by Automate Your Journey. This page explains what we collect through this site and how we use it.

What we collect

When you apply, we collect your name, email, mobile number, business name and type, location, years in business and anything you write in the form. We also record the page you applied from, any campaign tags in the link, and when and from where you gave consent to be contacted.

We use it to review your application, contact you about it by text, phone or email, and tell you about Journey Circles and related programs from Automate Your Journey. We store it in our customer relationship system.
