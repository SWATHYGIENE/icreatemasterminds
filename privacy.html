Privacy policy

iCreateMasterminds is operated by Automate Your Journey. This page explains what we collect through this site and how we use it.

What we collect

When you apply, we collect your name, email, mobile number, business name and type, location, years in business and anything you write in the form. We also record the page you applied from, any campaign tags in the link, and when and from where you gave consent to be contacted.

How we use it

We use it to review your application, contact you about it by text, phone or email, and tell you about iCreateMasterminds and related programs from Automate Your Journey. We store it in our customer relationship system.

Text messages

If you agreed to receive texts, message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help. We do not sell or share your mobile number or text-message consent with third parties for their marketing.

Connected circles and public feedback

Category circles share the Agrarian Exchange account and category system. Circle discussions remain visible to members who join that circle and administrators. My Reputation Pro hosts reviews and testimonials; its network panel displays only feedback authors have chosen to share publicly across the connected network. Choosing a feedback link takes you to My Reputation Pro, where its account and privacy rules apply.

Optional AI guidance

The AI helper sends only your selected public topic code to Agrarian Exchange and the shared My Reputation Pro service, which requests guidance from OpenAI using a predefined public prompt. It does not read your application, circle posts or account details. Generated drafts can be edited locally and are not submitted automatically.
